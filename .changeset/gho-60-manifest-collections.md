Publish component manifest collections. `@ghostvue/vue/manifest.json` and
`@ghostvue/themes/manifest.json` export every component manifest of the package,
unchanged and sorted by component name, in a versioned envelope. The envelope
schema is published as `@ghostvue/core/manifest-collection.schema.json`; the
single-component schema is unchanged.
