Add `GhostSkeleton` and `useGhostSkeleton` as the non-interactive loading
placeholder. The unstyled primitive is `aria-hidden` and `inert` so table and
toolbar bones stay silent. The themed wrapper adds a `shape` hook (`text`,
`rect`, `circle`), shimmer that stops under reduced motion, and token-only
visuals.
