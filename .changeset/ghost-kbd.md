Add `GhostKbd` and `useGhostKbd` for inline keyboard-shortcut display. The
unstyled component uses native `<kbd>` markup, renders key combinations, and
resolves platform modifier labels without reading `navigator` during setup. The
themed wrapper adds keycap chrome and a `size` prop.
