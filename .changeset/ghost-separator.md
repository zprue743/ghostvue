Add `GhostSeparator` and `useGhostSeparator` as the unstyled divider primitive,
plus a themed wrapper. Horizontal rules default to native `hr`; vertical rules
default to `div` with `role="separator"` and `aria-orientation="vertical"`.
Set `decorative` to omit the line from the accessibility tree.
