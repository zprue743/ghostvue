Add `GhostProgress` and `useGhostProgress` as the determinate progress
indicator. The unstyled component is a named `progressbar` with `value` / `max`
and an indeterminate fallback when the current value is unknown. The themed
wrapper adds `size` and `tone` and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
