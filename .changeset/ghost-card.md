Add `GhostCard` and `useGhostCard` as a composable surface with header, content,
and footer parts. Optional spotlight composes `useGhostInteractiveSurface` with
`data-interactive-state`. Themed `GhostCard` adds token-only elevation chrome
and `interactive="spotlight"`.
