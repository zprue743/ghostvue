Add `GhostSwitch` and `useGhostSwitch` as the unstyled on/off primitive, plus a
themed wrapper. The host is `button[role=switch]`; Space and Enter toggle;
named switches submit `name=value` while on and restore the mounted default on
form reset. `disabled` and `readonly` are distinct availability states.
