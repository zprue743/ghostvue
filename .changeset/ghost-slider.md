Add `GhostSlider` and `useGhostSlider` as the headless range input with APG
slider semantics, optional two-thumb `range` mode, stepped keyboard interaction,
orientation and RTL support, and hidden inputs for native forms. The themed
wrapper adds track, fill, and thumb recipes with `size` and `tone`.
