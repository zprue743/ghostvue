Add headless `GhostToastProvider`, `GhostToastViewport`, `GhostToast`, `GhostToastTitle`, `GhostToastDescription`, `GhostToastAction`, `GhostToastClose`, and `useGhostToast`. The provider owns a provider-scoped store (no module-global queue): at most `limit` toasts (default 3) are visible and later ones wait in FIFO order; informational toasts auto-dismiss after 8 seconds by default, while errors and toasts with actions persist. Timers pause on hover, focus within, and a hidden page. The viewport is a named `role="region"` with `data-ghost-live` and twin visually hidden announcers: ordinary toasts announce politely once when shown, and only `urgency: 'urgent'` uses the assertive region. Toasts never take focus; `Escape` dismisses only the focused toast without closing a dialog behind it and returns focus to where it was. `toast()` during SSR is a no-op with a development warning, and client calls before the viewport mounts are shown when it mounts. New development diagnostics `GHOST006`–`GHOST011` cover toast misuse. Modal focus traps and outside-press dismissal now leave `[data-ghost-live]` regions operable, as ADR 0012 describes.
